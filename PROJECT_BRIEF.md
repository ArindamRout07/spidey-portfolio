## 08 — Motion Direction

Motion should feel:

- cinematic
- physical
- intentional
- smooth
- slightly unpredictable

Avoid:

- excessive bouncing
- random animations
- constant movement
- unnecessary glow
- generic UI animation

---

## 09 — Signature Experience

The portfolio should contain a transformation
from a superhero-inspired visual identity into
my real identity.

This should eventually become an original
"web-to-self" interaction rather than a direct
copy of copyrighted character imagery.

---

## 10 — Design Philosophy

Do not create a generic developer portfolio.

Do not rely on cards.

Do not overuse gradients.

Do not animate everything.

Every visual effect must have a reason.
